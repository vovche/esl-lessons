## R · Прочитай

Two teams took part and the reporter checked the score before publication. A prize was given for teamwork, not only for winning. Students could also help as scorekeepers or scorekeepers. The report describes actions without insulting the other team. The nurse listens and asks one question at a time. This is language practice: students do not diagnose illness or suggest medicine.

## Додаток L — ВІДДІЛИТИ ДО СЛУХАННЯ

Повний текст для вчителя та подальшої паперової роботи. Самостійне читання цього додатка не є доказом слухання.

Mia watched a comedy about two lost robots in the school theatre on Friday at 5 p.m. The plot was very clear, but the performance was very lively. The audience could read a written summary on a screen. Mia found the first half more interesting than the second half. A friend preferred the ending because it was less predictable. Their review compares the story and the acting. Both opinions are accepted when they include a reason and respectful language. Mia is planning an imaginary trip. The group meets at the old castle gate on Saturday at 10 a.m. The itinerary includes a castle and a small museum. Both attractions are shown on a paper map. Each visitor receives a route card, but no online ticket is needed for this classroom story. The tour guide suggests either a short route or a longer route. Mia chooses the accessible route because it includes a quiet stop. The group checks the meeting point before leaving.
