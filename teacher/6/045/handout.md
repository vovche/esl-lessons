## Блок 1: How to get there

### 1. Словник

crossing — перехід; footbridge — пішохідний міст; opposite — навпроти; behind — позаду; tunnel — тунель; petrol — бензин

Опора (можна відкрити): Для маршруту використовуй наказову форму: Go straight on. Turn left/right. Go past the bank. Прийменники місця: next to, opposite, in front of, behind. Рух: into — усередину, out of — назовні, through — крізь, across — через, along/down — уздовж.

Мовна модель: Go straight on and turn right at the traffic lights. The bank is on your left, opposite the park.

### 2. Прочитай

Two friends are playing a game. One asks how to get to the bank. His friend tells him to go straight on and turn right at the traffic lights. Later they go past a bus stop and turn left. They stop in front of a supermarket. The house they need is behind it.
