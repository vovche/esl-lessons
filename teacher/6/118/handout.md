## R — розуміння прочитаного

The Green Stars are a fictional school band. They have played together for four years. Last week they performed in the town library. Their new song was written by Mia. The audience liked the quiet music. A school magazine published an interview with the band. Next month the students are going to make a short music video.

1. How long have they played together?

A) For three years. / B) For four years. / C) For two years.

Відповідь: __________

2. Where did they perform?

A) In the town library. / B) In the school garden. / C) In the town park.

Відповідь: __________

3. Who wrote the song?

A) Eva. / B) Anna. / C) Mia.

Відповідь: __________

4. What did the audience like?

A) The new shoes. / B) The quiet music. / C) The sports match.

Відповідь: __________

5. What published the interview?

A) A school magazine. / B) A travel guide. / C) A sports poster.

Відповідь: __________

6. What is their next plan?

A) Leave the band. / B) Open a shop. / C) Make a short music video.

Відповідь: __________
