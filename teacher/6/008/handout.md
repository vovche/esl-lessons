## Блок 1: Character

### 1. Словник і опора

skirt — спідниця; pocket — кишеня; patch — латка; butterfly — метелик; awful — жахливий; trainers — кросівки

Present Simple — звичка: usually, often, every day. Present Continuous — дія зараз або тимчасова ситуація: now, at the moment, these days. Дієслова like, love, want, know, need, understand зазвичай вживаємо у Simple, навіть коли говоримо про теперішнє.

Мовна модель: I usually wear jeans. Today I am making a skirt. I want a blue patch. I like the new design.

### 2. Прочитай

A girl usually buys her clothes, but today she is changing an old pair of jeans into a skirt. She is drawing a butterfly on a pocket. The drawing looks awful, so her friend gives her a patch. They put it over the drawing and make a new design together.
