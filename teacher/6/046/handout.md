## Блок 1: City vs countryside

### 1. Словник і опора

peaceful — спокійний; noisy — галасливий; countryside — сільська місцевість; caravan — будинок на колесах; houseboat — плавучий будинок; relaxed — розслаблений

Порівняння: short adjective + -er + than; more + long adjective + than. Big → bigger, easy → easier. Good → better, bad → worse. As + прикметник + as показує рівність; not as… as — менший ступінь ознаки. Після as не використовуй -er.

Мовна модель: The country is quieter than the city. The city is more exciting. This house is as comfortable as that one.

### 2. Прочитай

Some people enjoy a busy city with shops and restaurants. Other people prefer the countryside because it is quieter and more peaceful. A houseboat can be cheaper than a large house. A caravan lets people travel with their home. The best choice depends on the person’s interests and daily life.
