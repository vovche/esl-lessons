## Блок 1: TV programmes

### 1. Словник і опора

contestant — учасник конкурсу; channel — канал; documentary — документальна програма; cartoon — мультфільм; talent — талант; host — ведучий

Present Perfect: have/has + третя форма дієслова. Для досвіду без конкретного завершеного часу використовуй ever/never/before. Запитання: Have you ever…? Have seen, have watched, has won. Не поєднуй цей час із yesterday або last week.

Мовна модель: Have you ever watched a talent show? I have seen it twice. She has never won a competition.

### 2. Прочитай

Two boys are contestants on a game show. They must answer five questions about their friends to win a prize. One boy answers too quickly and makes mistakes. They answer only three questions correctly. They do not win, even though the other boy knew more answers. Listening to each other would have helped.
