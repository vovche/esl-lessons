## 4. 2 бали

Яке речення пояснює власний вибір без осуду інших?

1. Everyone must wear high heels.
2. A person in jeans is careless.
3. I choose flat shoes because I can dance in them.
4. Expensive clothes always mean kindness.

**Ключ: 3. I choose flat shoes because I can dance in them.**

## 5. 2 бали

Прочитай: Arron wears a kilt and a white shirt for Burns Night. Який висновок підтверджує текст?

1. Arron wears a kilt for this particular celebration.
2. Every person always wears a kilt.
3. Arron wears only sports clothes.
4. Burns Night is a school uniform.

**Ключ: 1. Arron wears a kilt for this particular celebration.**
