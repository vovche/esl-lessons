## 6. 3 бали

Прочитай текст. Dan’s tablet stops working while he is preparing a biography chart about a scientist. Emma suggests checking the battery and restarting the device. Dan saves his work, switches the tablet off and charges it. When it works again, he organises the chart by dates, discoveries and their importance. He checks the information in more than one source.

How does he organise the biography chart?

1. by prices of devices
2. by dates, discoveries and importance
3. by favourite colours

**Ключ: 2. by dates, discoveries and importance**
