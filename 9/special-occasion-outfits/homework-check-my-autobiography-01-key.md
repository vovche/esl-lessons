## 4. 2 бали

Яке речення логічно поєднує дитячий досвід із теперішньою звичкою?

1. I loved books as a child, and I still read every week.
2. I loved books, so yesterday tomorrow.
3. I read tomorrow last year.
4. Books are good good good.

**Ключ: 1. I loved books as a child, and I still read every week.**

## 5. 2 бали

Який фрагмент показує рису характеру через конкретний приклад?

1. I am creative. I am many things.
2. I have a name and a school.
3. I am creative because I write stories and draw my own characters.
4. I do things because things are things.

**Ключ: 3. I am creative because I write stories and draw my own characters.**
