## 4. 2 бали

Прочитай: Sofia makes her own clothes. Abdul trains hard and plays basketball. Який висновок має прямий доказ у тексті?

1. Sofia is creative, and Abdul is energetic.
2. Sofia dislikes designing clothes.
3. Abdul never exercises.
4. Both people only enjoy computer games.

**Ключ: 1. Sofia is creative, and Abdul is energetic.**

## 5. 2 бали

Прочитай: Diana likes jazz and plays guitar. Ajax enjoys playing the drums. Яка тема допоможе їм почати розмову?

1. Fashion design
2. Basketball training
3. Music and instruments
4. Their home addresses

**Ключ: 3. Music and instruments**
