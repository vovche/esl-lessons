**Допустимі відповіді:** "self-development"

## 3. 1 бал

**Допустимі відповіді:** "bookworm"

## 4. 2 бали

Який план є конкретною дією для розвитку мовлення?

1. I will compare myself with everyone.
2. I will buy new shoes.
3. I will never make mistakes.
4. I will practise speaking English for ten minutes with a partner every day.

**Ключ: 4. I will practise speaking English for ten minutes with a partner every day.**

## 5. 2 бали

Яке твердження про характер відповідає уроку?

1. Each person has only one fixed trait.
2. People can have a mix of traits, and some habits can change through practice.
3. Introverts can never speak confidently.
4. Only popular people can be role models.

**Ключ: 2. People can have a mix of traits, and some habits can change through practice.**
