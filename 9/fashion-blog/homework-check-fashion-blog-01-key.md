## 5. 2 бали

Яке речення обережно порівнює минулу і сучасну моду, уникаючи узагальнення про всіх?

1. Everyone wore bright clothes in the 1980s.
2. Nobody wears old styles today.
3. Some people wore leg warmers in the 1980s, and some wear them again today.
4. Everyone has always worn the same style.

**Ключ: 3. Some people wore leg warmers in the 1980s, and some wear them again today.**

## 6. 3 бали

2. In my opinion, comfort matters. For example, I choose trainers because I walk a lot. All in all, comfortable clothes suit my life.
3. Everyone must buy the same clothes. No reasons are needed.
4. My bag is blue. Tomorrow is Monday. The shop opens at nine.

**Ключ: 2. In my opinion, comfort matters. For example, I choose trainers because I walk a lot. All in all, comfortable clothes suit my life.**
