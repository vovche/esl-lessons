If they had checked them, they would have arrived earlier. They still enjoyed the waterfront and a local exhibition. Dan now recommends checking the timetable before visiting and leaving extra time for transport.

What does Dan recommend?

1. visiting only closed places
2. checking the timetable and allowing extra travel time
3. travelling without a plan

**Ключ: 2. checking the timetable and allowing extra travel time**
