## 4. 2 бали

Що є спостереженням, яке можна перевірити, а не оцінкою характеру?

1. The person is lazy because of the hoodie.
2. The hoodie is blue and loose.
3. Only formal clothes show respect.
4. The suit proves the person is kind.

**Ключ: 2. The hoodie is blue and loose.**

## 5. 2 бали

Чому однотонна сорочка може входити до casual і formal комплекту?

1. Because all shirts are uniforms.
2. Because style never depends on context.
3. Because the rest of the outfit and the occasion change its look.
4. Because every shirt is equally expensive.

**Ключ: 3. Because the rest of the outfit and the occasion change its look.**
