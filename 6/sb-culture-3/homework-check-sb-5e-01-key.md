## 4. 1 бал

Choose an invitation.

1. I cannot come tomorrow.
2. Thank you for inviting me.
3. Would you like to come to our event?

**Ключ: 3. Would you like to come to our event?**

## 5. 1 бал

Choose a polite acceptance.

1. I cannot make it.
2. I would love to come.
3. You must not come.

**Ключ: 2. I would love to come.**

## 6. 1 бал

Which detail should an invitation include?

1. The place and time.
2. Only the writer's favourite colour.
3. Only a goodbye.

**Ключ: 1. The place and time.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Our youth club is organising a tree-planting event. We are going to meet at the club at eight and walk to the park. We will plant trees in the morning and have a meal together later. I hope you can join us. If you decide to come, please send a reply.

When will the group meet?

1. At noon.
2. At ten at night.
3. At eight.

**Ключ: 3. At eight.**
