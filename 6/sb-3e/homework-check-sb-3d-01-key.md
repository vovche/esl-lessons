## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Two men were travelling through a cold forest with their dogs. Wolves were following them. One wolf looked different because it was half dog. A dog ran towards it, and other wolves appeared. One man tried to chase them, but he did not return. The other man felt alone and afraid.

Why did one wolf look different?

1. It was wearing a coat.
2. It had no legs.
3. It was half dog.

**Ключ: 3. It was half dog.**

## 8. 2 бали
