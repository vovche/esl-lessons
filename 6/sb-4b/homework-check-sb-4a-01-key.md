## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A boy wants to borrow a book from the library, but he has left his card at home. The librarian says he cannot borrow it without a card. She suggests asking a friend to borrow the book for him. At another shop, a girl wants a newspaper and a magazine. The shopkeeper checks the shelf.

Why cannot the boy borrow the book himself?

1. The library is closed.
2. He cannot read.
3. He has no library card with him.

**Ключ: 3. He has no library card with him.**
