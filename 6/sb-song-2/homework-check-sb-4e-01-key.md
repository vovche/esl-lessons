## 6. 1 бал

1. I think this city is beautiful.
2. There are two bridges.
3. The park opens at nine.

**Ключ: 1. I think this city is beautiful.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: I live in a city with a river and several bridges. There are museums, art galleries and a large stadium. Many visitors enjoy the view from the old bridge. My favourite place is the park because my friends and I can cycle there. I think our city is a great place to live.

Where does the writer like cycling?

1. At the airport.
2. Inside a museum.
3. In the park.

**Ключ: 3. In the park.**
