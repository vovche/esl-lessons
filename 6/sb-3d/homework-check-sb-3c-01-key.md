Прочитай текст: Two boys went on a water ride at a funfair. One boy was eating a sandwich when the boat went down a hill. Ketchup got on his shirt. The boat splashed into water, and the water washed some ketchup off. There were no roller coasters, but the boys enjoyed the other rides.

2. A cake.
3. A sandwich.

**Ключ: 3. A sandwich.**
