## 4. 1 бал

## 6. 1 бал

1. had to
2. must to
3. have to

**Ключ: 1. had to**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A boy wants to recycle a bottle. His friend explains that the container is for glass, but his bottle is plastic. She tells him to rinse it and check the correct bin. She also gives him a leaflet about recycling. They agree that keeping the environment clean is something everyone can help with.

What is the boy's bottle made of?

1. Glass.
2. Metal.
3. Plastic.

**Ключ: 3. Plastic.**
