## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A sports club can have more than one team. It may include football, basketball and hockey teams. Supporters watch matches and celebrate trophies. A successful club can change managers and players over the years. When you describe a favourite team, explain where it plays and why you support it.

Can one club have several teams?

1. No, only one player.
2. No, only one team.
3. Yes, in different sports.

**Ключ: 3. Yes, in different sports.**
