## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A girl joined a tennis club after a friend gave her advice. She practises three times a week with another member. The coach says she is doing well. Last week she won her first match. In two weeks she is going to take part in a tournament. She writes to a friend to share the news.

How often does the girl practise?

1. Once a month.
2. Every hour.
3. Three times a week.

**Ключ: 3. Three times a week.**
