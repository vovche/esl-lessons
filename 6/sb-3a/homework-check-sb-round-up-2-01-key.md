## 6. 1 бал

Прочитай текст: A girl returned from a holiday in Egypt . She visited famous sights and went on a cruise. She also rode a camel. She was scared because the camel was tall, but she did not fall off. She bought souvenirs for her friends. She left her camera at home and could not show her photos.

Why was the girl scared?

1. She lost her passport.
2. The museum was closed.
3. The camel was tall.

**Ключ: 3. The camel was tall.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Two friends are preparing for a trip. They check their passports and pack one large suitcase and a small bag. They check the weather and decide to take jumpers. They can check in online and download their boarding passes. Their parents will drive them to the airport.

Why do the friends take jumpers?

1. They lost their bags.
2. They are buying souvenirs.
3. They checked the weather.

**Ключ: 3. They checked the weather.**

## 8. 2 бали

How did the class travel?
