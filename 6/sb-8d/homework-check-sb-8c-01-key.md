## 6. 1 бал

2. Reporters write articles.
3. Reporters are writing articles.

**Ключ: 1. Articles are written by reporters.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: First, writers and editors meet and discuss topics. Reporters write articles and interview people. Graphic designers make the layout. The magazine is checked before it is sent to the printers. Many copies are printed, packed and delivered to shops. Readers can then buy the new issue.

Who makes the layout?

1. Drivers.
2. Spectators.
3. Graphic designers.

**Ключ: 3. Graphic designers.**

## 8. 2 бали
