## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Two friends are preparing for a trip. They check their passports and pack one large suitcase and a small bag. They check the weather and decide to take jumpers. They can check in online and download their boarding passes. Their parents will drive them to the airport.

2. They are buying souvenirs.

**Ключ: 3. They checked the weather.**
