## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A band is waiting for an audition. Two members feel nervous because a good performance could lead to a television appearance. Another member reminds them that they have worked hard. They agree that they enjoy performing together. They decide to do their best and have fun, even if they do not get on television.

Why are two members nervous?

1. They lost a suitcase.
2. The shop is closed.
3. The audition could lead to a TV appearance.

**Ключ: 3. The audition could lead to a TV appearance.**

## 8. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A band is waiting for an audition. Two members feel nervous because a good performance could lead to a television appearance. Another member reminds them that they have worked hard. They agree that they enjoy performing together. They decide to do their best and have fun, even if they do not get on television.

What do they decide to do?

1. Leave without performing.
2. Do their best and have fun.
3. Stop playing music.

**Ключ: 2. Do their best and have fun.**
