## 1. 1 бал

She ___ going to visit the fair.

1. are
2. does
3. is

**Ключ: 3. is**

## 2. 1 бал

I think phones ___ change in the future.

1. will
2. are
3. did

**Ключ: 1. will**

## 3. 1 бал

A rule forbids dropping rubbish. You ___ drop it.

1. may
2. must not
3. do not have to

**Ключ: 2. must not**

## 4. 1 бал

If we ___ the water, animals will suffer.

1. will pollute
2. polluted
3. pollute

**Ключ: 3. pollute**

## 5. 1 бал

Choose an invitation.

1. Would you like to come to our event?
2. I cannot come tomorrow.
3. Thank you for inviting me.

**Ключ: 1. Would you like to come to our event?**

## 6. 1 бал

Прочитай текст: Two friends want to visit a technology fair at the weekend. They are going to look at new computers and game consoles. One friend needs a keyboard because the old one does not work. They are meeting at the entrance at ten. They are not going to buy everything they see.

What does one friend need?

1. A suitcase.
2. A bicycle.
3. A keyboard.

**Ключ: 3. A keyboard.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A boy wants to recycle a bottle. His friend explains that the container is for glass, but his bottle is plastic. She tells him to rinse it and check the correct bin. She also gives him a leaflet about recycling. They agree that keeping the environment clean is something everyone can help with.

What is the boy's bottle made of?

1. Glass.
2. Metal.
3. Plastic.

**Ключ: 3. Plastic.**

## 8. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Our youth club is organising a tree-planting event. We are going to meet at the club at eight and walk to the park. We will plant trees in the morning and have a meal together later. I hope you can join us. If you decide to come, please send a reply.

When will the group meet?

1. At noon.
2. At ten at night.
3. At eight.

**Ключ: 3. At eight.**
