## 6. 1 бал

Прочитай текст: A boy wants to borrow a book from the library, but he has left his card at home. The librarian says he cannot borrow it without a card. She suggests asking a friend to borrow the book for him. At another shop, a girl wants a newspaper and a magazine. The shopkeeper checks the shelf.

Why cannot the boy borrow the book himself?

1. The library is closed.
2. He cannot read.
3. He has no library card with him.

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Some people enjoy a busy city with shops and restaurants. Other people prefer the countryside because it is quieter and more peaceful. A houseboat can be cheaper than a large house. A caravan lets people travel with their home. The best choice depends on the person’s interests and daily life.

Why do some people prefer the countryside?

1. It has more traffic.
2. It is always crowded.
3. It is quieter and more peaceful.

## 8. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: I live in a city with a river and several bridges. There are museums, art galleries and a large stadium. Many visitors enjoy the view from the old bridge. My favourite place is the park because my friends and I can cycle there. I think our city is a great place to live.

Where does the writer like cycling?

1. At the airport.
2. Inside a museum.
3. In the park.
