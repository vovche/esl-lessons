## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Two friends were exploring a snowy area when they saw huge footprints. The footprints looked like human footprints, but they were much bigger. The friends were frightened and ran back to the campsite. Other people thought a wild animal made them. The friends could not explain what they saw.

What was unusual about the footprints?

1. They were blue.
2. They were inside a bus.
3. They were much bigger than human footprints.

**Ключ: 3. They were much bigger than human footprints.**
