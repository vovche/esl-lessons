## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Two boys are contestants on a game show. They must answer five questions about their friends to win a prize. One boy answers too quickly and makes mistakes. They answer only three questions correctly. They do not win, even though the other boy knew more answers. Listening to each other would have helped.

How many correct answers did the boys need?

1. Three.
2. Two.
3. Five.

**Ключ: 3. Five.**

## 8. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Two boys are contestants on a game show. They must answer five questions about their friends to win a prize. One boy answers too quickly and makes mistakes. They answer only three questions correctly. They do not win, even though the other boy knew more answers. Listening to each other would have helped.

Why did they fail to win?

1. They left early.
2. One boy answered too quickly and made mistakes.
3. The show had no prize.

**Ключ: 2. One boy answered too quickly and made mistakes.**
