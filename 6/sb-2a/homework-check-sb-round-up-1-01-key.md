## 6. 1 бал

Прочитай текст: Our school has a theatre and a science lab. We study the usual subjects, but we also learn dance and music. Classes start at nine and finish at four. After school, some students practise in the theatre. I enjoy biology because I like learning about animals.

Where do some students practise after school?

1. In the kitchen.
2. In the airport.
3. In the theatre.

**Ключ: 3. In the theatre.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A girl usually buys her clothes, but today she is changing an old pair of jeans into a skirt. She is drawing a butterfly on a pocket. The drawing looks awful, so her friend gives her a patch. They put it over the drawing and make a new design together.

What is the girl making?

1. A jacket.
2. A cap.
3. A skirt.

**Ключ: 3. A skirt.**

## 8. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: I am tall and have got curly dark hair. I am active and outgoing, and I love sports. My best friend is short and has got blue eyes. He is clever and helpful, so I like studying with him. We both play ice hockey for a local team.

Why does the writer like studying with the friend?

1. The friend has blue eyes.
2. The friend is very tall.
3. The friend is clever and helpful.

**Ключ: 3. The friend is clever and helpful.**
