## 6. 1 бал

Прочитай текст: Two boys are contestants on a game show. They must answer five questions about their friends to win a prize. One boy answers too quickly and makes mistakes. They answer only three questions correctly. They do not win, even though the other boy knew more answers. Listening to each other would have helped.

How many correct answers did the boys need?

1. Three.
2. Two.
3. Five.

**Ключ: 3. Five.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: People sometimes shorten words in messages to type faster. They also use symbols to show feelings. A short message can be useful, but it must be clear to the receiver. A note about a borrowed bike should say who took it and when it will be returned. Clarity matters more than using many symbols.

Why do people shorten words?

1. To make every message longer.
2. To hide all meaning.
3. To type faster.

**Ключ: 3. To type faster.**

## 8. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A student used to help a younger brother with all his homework. Now the student has exams and cannot help all the time. The brother gets upset and receives lower marks. An advice writer suggests explaining the situation and showing the brother how to study independently. The student also needs time for their own work.

Why can the student not help all the time?

1. The student is on a cruise.
2. The brother moved away.
3. The student has exams.

**Ключ: 3. The student has exams.**
