## 1. 1 бал

How ___ apples have we got?

1. much
2. little
3. many

**Ключ: 3. many**

## 2. 1 бал

You should ___ an adult how you feel.

3. telling

**Ключ: 1. tell**

## 3. 1 бал

1. is she

## 6. 1 бал

Прочитай текст: A balanced diet can include different kinds of food. Fruit and vegetables provide vitamins. Dairy products contain protein and calcium. Some fast food contains a lot of salt, sugar or fat. A student compares two meals and notices that one has more vegetables. The student chooses that meal and drinks water.

What do dairy products contain in the text?

1. Only sugar.
2. No protein.
3. Protein and calcium.

**Ключ: 3. Protein and calcium.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Two friends visit a gym. One likes using the machines and lifting weights. The other prefers an aerobics class and wants advice from an instructor. They discuss different ways to exercise. They agree that a person should choose an activity they enjoy and can practise regularly.

Who wants advice from an instructor?

1. A bus driver.
2. A librarian.
3. The friend who prefers aerobics.

**Ключ: 3. The friend who prefers aerobics.**

## 8. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A girl joined a tennis club after a friend gave her advice. She practises three times a week with another member. The coach says she is doing well. Last week she won her first match. In two weeks she is going to take part in a tournament. She writes to a friend to share the news.

How often does the girl practise?

1. Once a month.
2. Every hour.
3. Three times a week.

**Ключ: 3. Three times a week.**
