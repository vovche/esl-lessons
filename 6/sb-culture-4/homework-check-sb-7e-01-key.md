## 4. 1 бал

Choose a natural phrase.

1. An advice.
2. Many advices.
3. Some advice.

**Ключ: 3. Some advice.**

## 5. 1 бал

I think you should ___ the problem.

1. explaining
2. explain
3. to explain

**Ключ: 2. explain**

## 6. 1 бал

A useful advice letter should…

1. Describe the problem and ask a clear question.
2. Give only a name.
3. Hide every important detail.

**Ключ: 1. Describe the problem and ask a clear question.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A student used to help a younger brother with all his homework. Now the student has exams and cannot help all the time. The brother gets upset and receives lower marks. An advice writer suggests explaining the situation and showing the brother how to study independently. The student also needs time for their own work.

Why can the student not help all the time?

1. The student is on a cruise.
2. The brother moved away.
3. The student has exams.

**Ключ: 3. The student has exams.**

## 8. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A student used to help a younger brother with all his homework. Now the student has exams and cannot help all the time. The brother gets upset and receives lower marks. An advice writer suggests explaining the situation and showing the brother how to study independently. The student also needs time for their own work.

What does the advice writer suggest?

1. Stop speaking to him.
2. Show the brother how to study independently.
3. Do every task for him forever.

**Ключ: 2. Show the brother how to study independently.**
