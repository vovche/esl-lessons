## 2. 1 бал

## 3. 1 бал

## 4. 1 бал

Choose a polite request for repetition.

1. Repeat now, you!
2. You repeating?
3. Could you repeat that, please?

**Ключ: 3. Could you repeat that, please?**

## 5. 1 бал

The jacket is too small. Ask for…

1. a quieter one.
2. a larger one.
3. a smaller receipt.

**Ключ: 2. a larger one.**

## 6. 1 бал

After a first role play, partners should…

1. change roles.
2. hide all answers forever.
3. stop listening.

**Ключ: 1. change roles.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Two partners practise a shopping dialogue. One is a customer and needs a jacket. The other is a shop assistant. The customer asks about size and price. The assistant offers another jacket when the first one is too small. They change roles and practise again, using a different item.

What does the customer need?

1. A passport.
2. A drum.
3. A jacket.

**Ключ: 3. A jacket.**

## 8. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: Two partners practise a shopping dialogue. One is a customer and needs a jacket. The other is a shop assistant. The customer asks about size and price. The assistant offers another jacket when the first one is too small. They change roles and practise again, using a different item.

What do they ask about?

1. Only a film.
2. Size and price.
3. Only the weather.

**Ключ: 2. Size and price.**
