## 6. 1 бал

**Ключ: 1. a source.**

## 7. 2 бали

Прочитай текст: A student wants to make a poster about a favourite sport. First, the student writes a plan with three headings: history, equipment and rules. Next, they collect information and note the sources. They write a draft, check the facts and spelling, and improve the layout. Finally, they explain the poster to a partner.

What are the poster's three headings?

1. Food, weather and travel.
2. Films, songs and shopping.
3. History, equipment and rules.

**Ключ: 3. History, equipment and rules.**
