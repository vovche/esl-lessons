01 Розберися

Натисни підкреслене англійське слово, щоб побачити переклад і послухати чотири голоси.

Правило й приклади

Determiners уточнюють іменник: a/the, this/those, my/their, another, other, others. Other стоїть перед іменником; others його замінює. Conjunctions поєднують думки: and, but, because, either ... or, neither ... nor.

This leaflet shows the castle. We can visit either the museum or the park. I need another ticket. Some attractions are better than others.

Прочитай і знайди факти

I can now talk about travel, landmarks and routes. I test myself without looking at the examples first. Then I compare my answers with the models and correct mistakes. I choose one speaking task and one written task to show what I can do. My next step is to practise the skill that still feels difficult.
